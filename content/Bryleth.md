# Bryleth

**Race/Class:** Fierna Tiefling / Druid
**Faction:** [Spree Alliance](../Factions/Spree-Alliance)
**Status:** Alive — active in the High Forest
**Relationship:** Hostile

---

## What You Know

Bryleth is a Spree Alliance operative and a serious threat in Chapter 4. A Fierna Tiefling druid with olive-green skin, prominent horns, long grey hair, and dark intense eyes. She looks like someone who chose the wilderness completely — not as a retreat, but as a weapon.

Her powers center on the land: roots and vines to entangle, animals and elementals to summon, weather control, earthquakes and landslides. She is territorial in the extreme. People who enter her domain uninvited disappear.

She calls herself the protector of the land, but her real motivation is revenge. The gods destroyed her home and killed everyone she loved.

---

## What She Wants

Vengeance against the gods. She will not stop. She will not negotiate on this point. Anyone who serves the gods, worships them, or benefits from their power is, to her, complicit.
