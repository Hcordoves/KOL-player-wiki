# Lord's Alliance

**Type:** Political and Military Coalition
**Status:** Weakening — losing ground across the North
**Headquarters:** [Waterdeep](../Locations/Waterdeep)
**Insignia:** Signet ring worn with the symbol on the palm side

---

## What You Know

The Lords' Alliance is the coalition that has kept civilization alive in the North for generations. An association of rulers — the lords of Waterdeep, Silverymoon, Yartar, and other free cities — who agreed that united they stand, divided they fall.

In 1508 DR, the Alliance is under serious pressure. The Spree Alliance has been attacking its cities systematically — Yartar was assaulted, Silverymoon's wards were destroyed, and the Alliance's most celebrated champion ([Mourn Eveningfall](../NPCs/Mourn-Eveningfall)) was gravely wounded in battle. Silverymoon is now under Fey Alliance control.

The Alliance continues to function. Waterdeep remains strong. But the direction is downward, and everyone who pays attention knows it.

---

## What They Want
- Keep the cities of the North safe and prosperous
- Maintain a united front against chaos and dark powers
- Eliminate threats proactively — before they reach the people
- Preserve the established order of civilization and the gods
