### Amaris Vendanii
**Player:** Kaylee | **Race/Class:** Aasimar / Paladin (Helm)

Raised in Silverymoon's House Invincible by her devout Helmite father. Excommunicated after her father was falsely accused. Her father died in poverty. Amaris survived on the streets, was found and trained by a Dragonborn paladin named Xissira. She took her oath seeking vengeance for her father's disgrace.

*Carries the Tome of the Undying Watch and a connection to Helm's power that she may not fully understand yet.*

---

### Dorroth of Catalonia (DOC)
**Player:** Kerry | **Race/Class:** Paladin (Zorquan)

Left his tribe to serve in the army. When he returned, his entire tribe had been wiped out. He followed a trail to a hidden temple and found Zorquan's holy symbol waiting for him. He still wears his wife's necklace. Always one to protect the helpless.

---

### Meranir
**Player:** Erin | **Race/Class:** Bard

Her mother was a great chieftess. Her father was a silver-tongued bard who slipped away before dawn. She discovered her bardic gift by accident, won a contest of wardrummers, and returned to her tribe in triumph. She returned to ashes. Now she wanders, looking for survivors and answers.
