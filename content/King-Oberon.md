# King Oberon

**Race/Class:** Archfey
**Faction:** [Fey Alliance](../Factions/Fey-Alliance)
**Status:** Alive — rules Silverymoon's North Bank
**Relationship:** Neutral

---

## What You Know

King Oberon is the co-ruler of the Fey Alliance alongside Queen Titania. He is the diplomatic face of the fey in Silverymoon — the one who meets with outsiders and projects an image of stability and reasonable governance.

He presents as measured, gracious, and approachable. He is the kind of ruler who makes you feel heard, which makes him effective and — if you're paying attention — slightly unnerving. You are rarely entirely sure what he wants from a conversation until it's over.

---

## What He Wants

Keep the fey clans unified and surviving until they can find a way home. Maintain Fey Alliance control of Silverymoon's North Bank. Stay neutral in the war between the Lord's Alliance and the Spree.

He is neutral by policy, not by nature. If the right offer came along, that neutrality would shift.

---

## How to Read Him

Oberon is the easier of the two fey rulers to deal with. What you get from him is honest as far as it goes — but it doesn't go all the way. For the full picture, you'd need Titania.
