# Yartar
![banner](banner.png)

**Region:** Dessarin Valley, Savage Frontier
**Status:** Rebuilding after the Battle of Yartar
**Controlling Faction:** [Lord's Alliance](../Factions/Lords-Alliance)

---

## What You Know

Yartar is a crossroads town — a place where honest merchants and dishonest ones, caravans and smugglers, mercenaries and spies all do business together. It sits on the Evermoor Way at the eastern bank of the River Surbrin.

The party has a history here. The Spree attacked Yartar. The party helped stop them. The city remembers.

**Current state (1508 DR):** Rebuilding. Old structures damaged in the battle are being torn down; new ones are rising. Northeast of the city, a **Tomb of the Unnamed Heroes** has been constructed — a memorial to the adventurers who gave their lives liberating Yartar. The party has a direct connection to this.

---

## Key Places

**The Fortified Bridge and Citadel** — The bridge connecting the city to its west-bank citadel. The Evermoor Way crosses here.

**Tomb of the Unnamed Heroes** — Northeast of the city. A permanent memorial to those who fell.

**The Surbrin Docks** — Trade barges move through at all hours. Heart of the city's commerce.

---

## Atmosphere

Resilient. Yartar has been through something hard and is choosing to rebuild rather than retreat. There's a grim civic pride here.
