# Waterdeep

**Region:** Sword Coast North
**Status:** Active
**Controlling Faction:** [Lord's Alliance](../Factions/Lords-Alliance) — The Lords of Waterdeep

---

## What You Know

Waterdeep is the City of Splendors. The Crown of the North. The most powerful and influential city in all of Faerûn. If something matters politically, commercially, or militarily in the North, Waterdeep is either involved or watching.

Over a million people call it home. A natural deep-water harbor made it the commercial hub of the Sword Coast. A government of mostly-masked lords — known only by the **Open Lord**, currently **Laeral Silverhand**. Beneath the city lies **Undermountain** — a vast dungeon complex.

Waterdeep remains strong while other Alliance cities are struggling. It is the most defensible and most resourced piece of the Lord's Alliance.

---

## Key People
- **Laeral Silverhand** — Open Lord; one of the Seven Sisters; Chosen of Mystra

---

## What It Means Right Now

For the current arc, Waterdeep is background — a distant power the party may eventually need to reach. If the crisis in the North escalates to the point where the party needs Alliance-level backing or real arcane resources, Waterdeep is where that happens.
