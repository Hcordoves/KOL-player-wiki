# Mourn Eveningfall

![Mourn Eveningfall](Mourn%20Eveningfall.jpeg)

**Race/Class:** High Elf / Paladin
**Faction:** Lord's Alliance — founder of the Knights of Legend
**Status:** Alive — gravely wounded; recovering in Silverymoon
**Relationship:** Friendly

---

## What You Know

Mourn Eveningfall is a legend. A High Elf paladin and General of the Armies of Faerûn, he fought in some of the most significant conflicts of the last few centuries. He rose from private to General, earned the title **Knight of ALL Realms** from Lady Alustriel Silverhand, and co-founded the Knights of Legend with his best friend Taeghen Moondown.

Tall, with long blond hair and piercing blue eyes. He wears full plate and carries a Moonblade called **Malevolent Defender**. His presence is commanding — the kind of person a room organizes itself around.

**Current status:** After battling the god Bane and the goddess Lolth directly during the Spree's invasion of Silverymoon, Mourn was gravely wounded. He is being cared for by Lady Alustriel Silverhand. He is out of the field.

---

## What He Wants

To retire. To settle in Silverymoon with Alustriel, raise a family, and hand the realm's problems to someone else. He stays because he believes it isn't safe to stop yet.
