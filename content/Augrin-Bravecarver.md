# Augrin Bravecarver

![Augrin Bravecarver](Augrin%20Bravecarver.jpeg)

**Race/Class:** Minotaur / Barbarian
**Faction:** Knights of Legend
**Status:** Missing — High Forest
**Relationship:** Friendly — current rescue target

---

## What You Know

Augrin Bravecarver is a Minotaur barbarian and member of the Knights of Legend — and the reason the party is in the High Forest in Chapter 4. He has gone missing, and recovering him is the current primary objective.

He is enormous. Covered in scars from years of gladiatorial combat. What he lacks in social refinement he makes up for in raw loyalty and physical capability.

Abandoned as a child, raised by wandering barbarians, kidnapped into slavery and the arena where he became a champion — suffering only one defeat: a loss to Taeghen Moondown, fighting in disguise as the Blue Wyvern. He was recruited and freed by Charles Vane, who brought him into the Knights of Legend.

---

## What He Wants

To be free. To find his tribe — the band of barbarians who raised him. He serves because it gives him purpose, not because he's compelled to.
