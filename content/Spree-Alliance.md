# The Spree Alliance

**Type:** Shadow Organization / Cult
**Status:** Expanding aggressively
**Known Leadership:** A figure called **Merrick** — identity unclear
**Known Strongholds:** Southwood, High Moors, Serpent Hills, High Forest

---

## What You Know

The Spree Alliance is the primary threat to the North in 1508 DR. They believe the gods are a cage and want to tear it all down. On the surface: a liberation movement. Underneath: a cult infrastructure funding lich transformations and guarding phylacteries.

They assaulted Yartar — the party helped repel them. They invaded Silverymoon — the city's magical wards were destroyed. That counts as a Spree strategic win.

---

## What They Want
- Eliminate the gods and dismantle divine infrastructure
- Replace the old order with self-governance
- Support the rise of dreadlords
- Spread their ideology through propaganda, infiltration, and force
