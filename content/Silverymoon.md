# Silverymoon

**Region:** Luruar, Northwest Faerûn
**Status:** Partially destroyed — Fey Alliance occupied
**Controlling Faction:** [Fey Alliance](../Factions/Fey-Alliance) (North Bank)

---

## What You Know

Silverymoon is called the Gem of the North, and even partially destroyed, you can see why. A city of magic, culture, and scholarship built to resemble the ancient elven city of Myth Drannor. The permanent Moonbridge — a structure of pure magic — spans the River Rauvin connecting its two halves.

**Current state (1508 DR):** The Spree Alliance invaded. Mourn Eveningfall fought Bane directly in the city's defense, and the battle destroyed the magical wards that had protected Silverymoon for centuries. The Fey Alliance moved in. King Oberon now rules the North Bank. Lady Alustriel Silverhand remains here, tending to the gravely wounded Mourn.

The city is not a warzone. The fey occupation is not brutal. But it is occupation, and the city's people are living under a new order they didn't choose.

---

## Key Places

**The High Palace** — Seat of government. Currently under Fey Alliance authority.

**The Moonbridge** — The magical bridge connecting Northbank and Southbank. Still standing.

**The Golden Oak Inn** — Rustic inn with a living oak tree through its atrium. Also a temple to Shiallia. Good place to hear gossip.

**The Dancing Goat** — Bawdy tavern on the Moonway. Loud, busy. Also: pickpockets. Also: allegedly, a secret door to passages beneath the city.

**The Wyrmworks** — Dragon parts shop. Survived the Spree invasion nearly untouched. The owners know a lot about dragon activity in the region.

**The House Invincible** — Former temple to Helm. Decommissioned. Its current status is unclear.

---

## Atmosphere

Cautious. The city is rebuilding, but the people are uncertain. The fey occupation isn't harsh, but the fey are strange and don't explain themselves. There's a sense that the crisis isn't over — it has just paused.
