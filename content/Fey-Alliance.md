# The Fey Alliance

**Type:** Displaced Fey Nation
**Status:** Active — controls Silverymoon's North Bank
**Leadership:** [King Oberon](../NPCs/King-Oberon) and [Queen Titania](../NPCs/Queen-Titania)
**Insignia:** The trinity knot
**Territory:** Silverymoon North Bank; High Forest enclave

---

## What You Know

The Fey Alliance isn't from here. A great magical disturbance collapsed the barriers between the Feywild and the prime material plane, and thousands of fey were suddenly thrust into a world they didn't choose.

Rather than scatter, they held together. Oberon and Titania established a hidden enclave in the High Forest. When Silverymoon's wards fell during the Spree invasion, the Fey Alliance moved in. Oberon now governs the North Bank.

The Fey Alliance is not your enemy — but they're not straightforwardly your ally either. Their one real priority is getting home to the Feywild. Everything else is negotiable.

---

## What They Want
- Survive in the prime material plane
- **Get back to the Feywild** — everything else serves this goal
- Maintain the balance of nature
- Build alliances until they can go home

---

## Leadership
- **[King Oberon](../NPCs/King-Oberon)** — The diplomat. The face of the Alliance.
- **[Queen Titania](../NPCs/Queen-Titania)** — The power. Sets the Alliance's true direction.
