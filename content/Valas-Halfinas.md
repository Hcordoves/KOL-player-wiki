# Valas Halfinas

![Valas Halfinas](Valas%20Halfinas.jpeg)

**Race/Class:** Fallen Aasimar / Vampire Lord
**Faction:** Spree Alliance
**Status:** Alive
**Relationship:** Hostile

---

## What You Know

Valas Halfinas is the primary villain of the campaign — the figure believed to lead the Spree Alliance under the alias **Merrick**. He is a Fallen Aasimar transformed into a Vampire Lord, responsible for the Spree's systematic war against the gods.

Tall, with light olive skin, light blue eyes, and short black hair. He wears blackened steel full plate with a cloak that seems to absorb light. He carries a longsword pulsating with dark energy and a tower shield trimmed with strips of human flesh.

---

## What He Wants

Valas wants to destroy the gods. He blames them for abandonment and betrayal. His goal: a world free from divine influence. The Spree Alliance is the instrument of that agenda.

He uses manipulation, terror, and the corruption of souls as his methods. He rarely shows his hand until he's already won.

---

## What's Known About His Past

Not much. He was once apparently a figure of some religious standing — a cleric, by his knowledge of divine systems. Something happened that turned him against the gods completely.

---

## What You Don't Know

His true history, how he became what he is, and who — if anyone — might be above him in the chain of power.
